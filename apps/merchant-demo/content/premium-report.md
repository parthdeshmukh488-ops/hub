# Budget e-bikes under €1,500: the 2026 comparison

*Leash demo research desk · premium report · September 2026*

## Summary

Six e-bikes under €1,500 were compared on the things that decide daily use: real-world range, braking, weight, service and five-year cost. The **Voltra City 3** is the best all-rounder for flat city commutes, the **Kestrel Trail 500** is the only sensible choice for hilly routes, and the **Fjord Fold 20** wins if the bike has to travel on trains or up stairs.

## How we compared

- **Range:** a 21 km mixed loop (city traffic, one 4% climb), rider plus luggage 90 kg, middle assist level, 14 °C. Repeated until the battery cut out.
- **Braking:** stopping distance from 25 km/h, dry and wet.
- **Weight:** measured with battery, without accessories.
- **Service:** distance to the nearest authorised workshop from five German cities, and whether spare batteries and displays are listed.
- **Five-year cost:** purchase price, one replacement battery, wear parts, yearly service and electricity.

## Best for flat city commutes: Voltra City 3

Quiet, well balanced and with the best brakes-to-price ratio in the test. The 504 Wh battery comfortably covers a week of 8 km commutes between charges. Spare batteries are listed for five years, and 4 of the 5 cities had a workshop within 10 km.

## Best for hills: Kestrel Trail 500

The only mid-drive in the band. On the 4% climb it held 22 km/h where the hub motors dropped to 15–17 km/h. The cost is faster chain wear (budget one extra chain a year) and a price at the top of the range.

## Best for trains and stairs: Fjord Fold 20

At 19.8 kg it is the only bike here you can carry up a flight of stairs without dreading it. The 360 Wh battery limits it to shorter trips; it is the right bike for a combined train-and-bike commute, not for 20 km each way.

## What to avoid

- **Mechanical discs on hilly routes:** the Nordwind and the Fjord needed noticeably longer to stop after the descent.
- **Front hub motors on wet cobbles:** the Riverline's front wheel slipped twice when starting uphill in the wet.
- **Brochure range figures:** every claimed figure was 25–45% above what we measured at the middle assist level.

## Five-year cost

Purchase price is only about 55–60% of what you will spend. The largest single item is the battery replacement (€350–650). The yearly service (€60–80) catches worn brake pads and chains before they damage more expensive parts. Electricity is negligible: about €5 per year for a daily 16 km round trip.

## Verdict

Buy the **Voltra City 3** unless your commute has real hills (then the **Kestrel Trail 500**) or has to fit on a train (then the **Fjord Fold 20**). Whichever you choose: test-ride your frame size, check the local workshop, and put a proper lock on the shopping list.
